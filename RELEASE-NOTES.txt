In the Hunt v0.9.17 for Windows x64

Experimental 16:9 widescreen

Choose Arcade for the original 4:3 view or Widescreen for the wider playfield.
Stage five keeps its original-width shaft in both modes.

This update fixes the placement of freeze-ray launchers, mounted enemies,
breakable scenery, and the stage-six lift. All six stage spawn tables were
checked against MAME 0.289.

Extract the entire ZIP and open InTheHunt.exe. On first launch, select your
own supported MAME ROM archive. To carry over saves and settings, copy
your existing .local/world folder into the new installation.
