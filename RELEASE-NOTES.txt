In the Hunt v0.9.21 for Windows x64

CRT curvature option

- New Options > Video > CURVATURE toggle (off by default).
- With a CRT shader selected, the shader's own tube curvature is used.
- Without a CRT shader, the picture gets a gentle curved tube face with
  smooth rounded corners; fine scrolling stays smooth and the picture is
  filtered so curved rows do not shimmer.
- The setting changes instantly and is saved with your other settings.

Extract the entire ZIP and open InTheHunt.exe. On first launch, select your
own supported MAME ROM archive. To carry over saves and settings, copy
your existing .local/world folder into the new installation.
