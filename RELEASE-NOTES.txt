In the Hunt v0.9.22 for Windows x64

Choose the screen mode when you play

- PLAY on the title menu now asks for a screen mode: 4:3 ARCADE or
  16:9 WIDESCREEN (EXPERIMENTAL). The game starts straight away in the
  chosen mode.
- The chooser remembers your last pick, and Back returns to the title menu.
- The ASPECT option has been removed from Options > Video.

Extract the entire ZIP and open InTheHunt.exe. On first launch, select your
own supported MAME ROM archive. To carry over saves and settings, copy
your existing .local/world folder into the new installation.
