In the Hunt v0.9.18 for Windows x64

Ultrawide (21:9) mode

- New Ultrawide mode beside Arcade and Widescreen: a 64:27 playfield (568 columns) for 21:9 monitors, chosen from the mode selector or with --mode=ultrawide.
- Scenery, enemies, HUD panels and menus fill the wider view on all six stages; each mode keeps its own high scores.
- Bosses now reach and shoot at subs camping in the far wings, including the stage-one claw submarine.
- Fixed the sub wrapping from the left wing to the right side of the screen.
- Wider stage-three boss wall; unwritten background cells in the wings render black instead of grey.
- New radiance-cascade bounce lighting, soft shadows and light rays in the GPU effects.
- Arcade mode output is unchanged.

Extract the entire ZIP and open InTheHunt.exe. On first launch, select your
own supported MAME ROM archive. To carry over saves and settings, copy
your existing .local/world folder into the new installation.
