In the Hunt v0.9.20 for Windows x64

Widescreen edge and placement fixes

- Stage 2 sunken city: the building towers no longer show dark blocks or
  notches; their sprite pieces now line up with the map art.
- Other scroll-locked scenery and hazards (sea-wall scenery, sunken-city
  turrets and pieces) keep their map position instead of shifting at the edge.
- Stage 1 boss cavern: the left edge no longer shows a hard seam.
- Stage 2 twin-hull arena: the wrong strip on the right edge is gone.
- Stage 4 lava cave: stale side patches no longer flash when the camera lock moves on.
- Arcade mode output is unchanged.

Extract the entire ZIP and open InTheHunt.exe. On first launch, select your
own supported MAME ROM archive. To carry over saves and settings, copy
your existing .local/world folder into the new installation.
