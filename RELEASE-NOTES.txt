In the Hunt v0.9.19 for Windows x64

Widescreen fixes and native ROM import

- ROM import now runs inside the game. It no longer writes a PowerShell script
  to the temp folder, which some antivirus engines falsely flagged.
  Existing imports keep working without re-importing.
- The 21:9 ultrawide mode is retired: Aspect is 4:3 or 16:9, and a saved
  ultrawide setting loads as 16:9.
- Widescreen bosses: the stage-one claw submarine's arm attack is back, the
  stage-four boss's flames reach into the wings, and no wing area is a safe zone.
- Widescreen visuals: stage-one far scenery, the stage-four arena and stage-five
  wings are filled; whales, mines and attract-mode wings stay drawn.
- Enhanced renderer: duplicated ice-mount sprites stay attached to the map.
- Options: the aspect label no longer clips, and fit scaling shows FIT.
- Arcade mode output is unchanged.

Extract the entire ZIP and open InTheHunt.exe. On first launch, select your
own supported MAME ROM archive. To carry over saves and settings, copy
your existing .local/world folder into the new installation.
