In the Hunt v0.9.18 for Windows x64

Widescreen (16:9) mode

- Scenery, enemies, HUD panels and menus fill the wider view on all six stages; each mode keeps its own high scores.
- Bosses reach and shoot at subs hugging the far edges, including the stage-one claw submarine and the stage-four boss's flames.
- Stage 5 fills the wide picture instead of black bars, and the stage-4 arena wings are solid scenery.
- The sub can no longer wrap from the left wing to the right side of the screen.
- Arcade mode output is unchanged.

Extract the entire ZIP and open InTheHunt.exe. On first launch, select your
own supported MAME ROM archive. To carry over saves and settings, copy
your existing .local/world folder into the new installation.
